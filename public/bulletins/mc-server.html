🔌 插件功能

服务器安装了以下插件，提供额外的游戏功能：

📑 插件目录

  • GSit - 姿态系统
  • BlockLocker - 容器锁定
  • VeinMiner - 连锁挖掘
  • ImageFrame - 图片展示
  • 传送指令
  • CoreProtect - 玩家操作记录
  • VoiceChat - 语音聊天
  • 其他功能

GSit - 姿态系统

  • /sit 或 /gsit → 坐在方块上
  • /lay 或 /glay → 躺在方块上
  • /bellyflop 或 /gbellyflop → 趴在方块上
  • /spin 或 /gspin → 在方块上转圈
  • /crawl 或 /gcrawl → 在地上爬行
  • /sit toggle 或 /sit playertoggle → 切换点击方块或玩家的功能
  • /crawl toggle → 切换使用再次潜行来爬行的功能

💡 提示：也可以右键板砖、楼梯坐下

BlockLocker - 容器锁定

  • 拿着告示牌对着容器直接右键可以锁定
  • 如果还要加人，可以右键告示牌，换行加可访问的人

VeinMiner - 连锁挖掘

  • 蹲下即可开关连锁挖掘功能
  • 只针对矿物和木头生效

ImageFrame - 图片展示

  • 可以将图片用地图的方式放到游戏中进行展示，具体使用方法请自行搜索相关教程

传送指令

  • /tpa → 请求传送到某玩家
  • /tpaall → 请求所有玩家传送到自己
  • /tpacancel → 取消传送请求
  • /tpaccept → 接受传送请求
  • /tpahere → 请求某玩家传送到自己

CoreProtect - 玩家操作记录

  • 可以查看玩家在服务器上的所有操作记录，包括但不限于破坏、放置、交互等
  • 如需查看记录，请使用指令 /coreprotect info 或 /co info，仅可查询，不可回溯

VoiceChat - 语音聊天

  • 客户端需要安装对应的 mod 即可在服务器内与同样安装了该 mod 的玩家进行语音通话

其他功能

  • /msg → 单独发送信息给某位玩家
  • /fly → 在生存模式下起飞
  • /help → 查看每个插件的使用方法

服务器准则

第一章 总则

  1. 第一条 为维护 SECTLCraft 服务器（以下简称"本服"）的游戏秩序，保障玩家合法权益，营造和谐、有序的游玩环境，特制定本条例。
  2. 第二条 凡进入本服游玩的玩家，均视为已充分阅读、理解并自愿接受本条例之约束。本条例效力涵盖游戏内、官方交流群及相关社区平台。
  3. 第三条 玩家应当遵守诚实信用原则。任何以"未认真阅读相关规定"为由请求减免处罚的，不予支持，并视情节予以从重处罚。

第二章 辅助功能与作弊判定

  1. 第四条 服务器准予玩家使用下列辅助类功能：
    • 矿物透视、实体高亮（ESP）、全亮度调节；
    • 自由相机（灵魂出窍）、自动钓鱼、投影打印；
    • 自动音符盒音乐演奏、自动点击（严禁用于 PvP 环境）。
  2. 第五条 除本条例第四条明文列举的功能外，其余具有破坏平衡性质的功能均在禁止之列。若玩家对特定功能之合规性存疑，应当先行向管理层申报咨询。
  3. 第六条 玩家明知他人实施违规作弊行为而利用其优势获利的，视为共同违规，按作弊行为追究责任。
  4. 第七条 管理人员使用创造模式仅限于公共工程建设及生物生成。严禁利用职权产出物资赠予他人。

第三章 资产权属与建设规范

  1. 第八条 玩家对其建设的建筑、机器及存放物资的容器享有所有权。建议玩家在显著位置设置权属标识牌，或通过 Bolt 进行技术锁定。
  2. 第九条 严禁未经所有权人许可，擅自拆除、修改他人建筑，或启停他人机器、挪用他人物资。自然生成的场景结构不适用本条规定。
  3. 第十条 资产权属的鉴定遵循下列顺位：
    • 优先以标识牌或其他明显登记之信息为准；
    • 若无上述信息，由管理人员调用 CoreProtect 记录鉴定。以 7 日内首位放置该方块（或核心组件）之玩家为所有人；
    • 若经上述手段仍无法核实，则视为无主资产，管理层有权依照相关程序处置。
  4. 第十一条 机器设备运行完毕后，操作人应当即时关闭。严禁建造危害服务器运行效能的设施。凡导致 MSPT > 60 或 TPS < 10 的机器，或利用超过 3 个假人载体的设施，均属违规。
  5. 第十二条 严禁建造导致他人客户端视觉卡顿的设施。凡导致他人 FPS 降幅超过 50% 的，管理层有权要求整改。

第四章 社区言论与社交规范

  1. 第十三条 严禁利用本服相关社群发布下列违法违规信息：
    • 涉及淫秽色情、赌博、毒品贩卖与交流等内容；
    • 涉及极端政治、意识形态对立等敏感言论。
    违反本条之处罚结果，不予申诉。
  2. 第十四条 严禁实施人身攻击、歧视或恶意引战行为。针对多人参与的争吵，管理层有权对全体参与者予以同等处罚。
  3. 第十五条 严禁因观点差异（如对动画评价、角色喜好等之争执）引发群体性互喷。
  4. 第十六条 严禁实施"开盒"等侵犯他人隐私之行为，包括但不限于推算他人真实姓名、传播未经授权的影像资料。
  5. 第十七条 玩家间 PvP 行为应当建立在双方明确同意的基础之上。严禁以恶意报复为目的损害他人权益，报复行为中的违规部分将作为加重处罚情节。

第五章 管理权限与程序

  1. 第十八条 管理人员行使职权应当遵循尊重隐私、预估风险及权责对等原则，以服务玩家为宗旨，严禁滥用职权、收受私利。
  2. 第十九条 管理决策实行管理层集体表决制度：
    • 封禁、禁言、申诉审批、小型回滚操作，需经 1/2 以上管理人员赞成；
    • 强制拆除资产、大型回滚操作，需经 2/3 以上管理人员赞成；
    • 全服回档或特大型运行操作，需经 2/3 以上管理人员赞成，并报请服主批准。
  3. 第二十条 服主对管理层的所有集体决议保留最终裁定权及否决权。

第六章 处罚与救济

  1. 第二十一条 违反本条例者，管理层有权视情节采取警告、撤销管理权限、阶梯式封禁或永久清退等处置措施。
  2. 第二十二条 玩家对处罚决定存有异议的，可通过本服规定的官方渠道发起申诉。
  3. 第二十三条 举报制度：
    • 举报人应当提供真实有效的证据材料；
    • 管理层对举报人身份实行严格保密机制；
    • 严禁多次恶意举报，违者取消举报资格并追究责任。

Server Rules

These rules may change at any time. The server owner's latest published version shall prevail.

Chapter 1: General Provisions

  1. Article 1 These regulations are formulated to maintain the game order of SECTLCraft server (hereinafter referred to as "this server"), safeguard the legitimate rights and interests of players, and create a harmonious and orderly gaming environment.
  2. Article 2 All players who enter this server for gaming are deemed to have fully read, understood, and voluntarily accepted the constraints of these regulations. The effectiveness of these regulations covers in-game, official communication groups, and related community platforms.
  3. Article 3 Players shall abide by the principle of good faith. Any request for reduction or exemption of punishment on the grounds of "not carefully reading relevant regulations" will not be supported, and may be subject to aggravated punishment depending on the circumstances.

Chapter 2: Auxiliary Features and Cheating Determination

  1. Article 4 The server allows players to use the following auxiliary features:
    • Ore X-ray, entity highlighting (ESP), full brightness adjustment;
    • Freecam (soul out-of-body), auto-fishing, projection printing;
    • Automatic note block music performance, auto-click (strictly prohibited in PvP environments).
  2. Article 5 Except for the features explicitly listed in Article 4 of these regulations, all other features that disrupt game balance are prohibited. If players have doubts about the compliance of specific features, they should first consult the management team.
  3. Article 6 Players who knowingly benefit from others' violation of cheating rules by exploiting their advantages will be regarded as joint violators and held accountable for cheating behavior.
  4. Article 7 Management personnel's use of creative mode is limited to public engineering construction and mob spawning. It is strictly prohibited to use authority to produce materials and give them to others.

Chapter 3: Property Ownership and Construction Standards

  1. Article 8 Players have ownership over buildings, machines, and containers storing materials they have built. It is recommended that players set up ownership signs in prominent locations, or use Bolt or WorldGuard for technical locking.
  2. Article 9 It is strictly prohibited to dismantle or modify others' buildings, or start or stop others' machines, or use others' materials without the permission of the owner. Naturally generated scene structures are not subject to this provision.
  3. Article 10 The determination of property ownership follows the following priority:
    • Priority is given to information on signs or other obvious registrations;
    • If there is no such information, management personnel shall verify using CoreProtect records. The player who first placed the block (or core component) within 7 days is considered the owner;
    • If ownership still cannot be verified through the above means, it is considered ownerless property, and the management team has the right to dispose of it according to relevant procedures.
  4. Article 11 After machine equipment operation is completed, the operator shall promptly turn it off. It is strictly prohibited to build facilities that harm server performance. Any machine that causes MSPT > 60 or TPS < 10, or facilities that use more than 3 fake player entities, are considered violations.
  5. Article 12 It is strictly prohibited to build facilities that cause visual lag for other clients. Any facility that causes others' FPS to drop by more than 50% may be subject to rectification requirements by the management team.

Chapter 4: Community Speech and Social Norms

  1. Article 13 It is strictly prohibited to use this server's related communities to publish the following illegal and prohibited information:
    • Content involving pornography, obscenity, gambling, drug trafficking and exchange, etc.;
    • Sensitive remarks involving extreme politics and ideological opposition.
    Punishment results for violating this article cannot be appealed.
  2. Article 14 It is strictly prohibited to engage in personal attacks, discrimination, or malicious provocation. For disputes involving multiple participants, the management team has the right to impose equal punishment on all participants.
  3. Article 15 It is strictly prohibited to trigger group mutual attacks due to differences in opinions (such as disputes over anime evaluations, character preferences, etc.).
  4. Article 16 It is strictly prohibited to engage in behaviors that infringe on others' privacy, such as "doxing," including but not limited to deducing others' real names, spreading unauthorized image materials.
  5. Article 17 PvP behavior between players should be based on explicit mutual consent. It is strictly prohibited to harm others' rights and interests for the purpose of malicious retaliation. Violations committed during retaliation will be considered as aggravating circumstances for punishment.

Chapter 5: Management Authority and Procedures

  1. Article 18 Management personnel exercising authority shall follow the principles of respecting privacy, assessing risks, and balancing rights and responsibilities, with serving players as the purpose. Abuse of power and acceptance of private benefits are strictly prohibited.
  2. Article 19 Management decisions implement a collective voting system by the management team:
    • Bans, mutes, appeal approvals, and small rollback operations require approval from more than 1/2 of management personnel;
    • Forced demolition of assets and large rollback operations require approval from more than 2/3 of management personnel;
    • Server-wide rollbacks or extra-large operational activities require approval from more than 2/3 of management personnel and must be reported to the server owner for approval.
  3. Article 20 The server owner reserves the final decision-making and veto power over all collective resolutions of the management team.

Chapter 6: Punishments and Remedies

  1. Article 21 Those who violate these regulations may be subject to disciplinary measures such as warnings, revocation of management permissions, tiered bans, or permanent removal, depending on the circumstances.
  2. Article 22 Players who have objections to punishment decisions may initiate appeals through the official channels specified by this server.
  3. Article 23 Reporting system:
    • Reporters shall provide true and valid evidence materials;
    • The management team implements a strict confidentiality mechanism for reporter identities;
    • Repeated malicious reporting is prohibited, and violators will lose reporting privileges and be held accountable.
